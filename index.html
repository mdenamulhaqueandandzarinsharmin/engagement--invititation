With the Blessings of Our Families

With hearts softened by gratitude, we turn to those who first taught us what love means: our parents, our elders, our family, and our friends. As we step into this sacred journey, we carry your prayers like a lantern, lighting the path ahead.

Every dua you whispered for us, every quiet sacrifice, every gentle hand that guided us here, all of it lives in this moment.
