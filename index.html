The Engagement

✦ ✦ ✦

With the blessings of Allah and the love of our families, two hearts are brought together to begin a beautiful journey of love, companionship, and faith.
On 27th December 2026, Md. Enamul Haque and Zarin Sharmin will take their first step toward a lifetime of love, togetherness, and cherished memories.

Your presence will make our joy complete, and your duas will make this beginning even more beautiful.
With open hearts and warm smiles, we welcome you to celebrate love, family, faith, and the beginning of a beautiful forever.

With the Blessings of Our Families

✦ ✦ ✦

There are moments in life that become beautiful because they are shared with the people we love.
With hearts filled with gratitude, we turn to those who first taught us the meaning of love— our parents, our elders, our family, and our friends.
Every dua whispered for us, every sacrifice made with love, and every gentle hand that guided us to this moment will forever remain a cherished part of our journey.
As we begin this new chapter, we pray that Allah fills our hearts with sakinah, our love with mawaddah, and our lives with rahmah.
May our future home be filled with peace, our days with barakah, our hearts with gratitude, and our journey always be guided towards Him.
May we grow together, pray together, stand beside one another through every season, and walk hand in hand towards a lifetime blessed by Allah.

“Our Lord, grant us from among our spouses and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us an example for the righteous.”
(Qur’an 25:74)
